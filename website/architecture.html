Architecture

CONNECT Framework follows a modular architecture that separates governance logic, enterprise inventory intelligence, and role-specific AI assistance.

Experience Layer

Web portal and AI assistant interfaces for executives, architects, engineers, and risk officers.

Governance Intelligence Layer

Governance Engine, Standardization Engine, and Consolidation Recommender provide decision support.

Platform Knowledge Layer

Tool Registry and Platform Services API Catalog create a trusted enterprise inventory baseline.

Integration Layer

Monitoring and CMDB connectors keep governance context synchronized with real operational data.

For deeper technical details, refer to docs/architecture.md.

Designed for incremental enterprise adoption.
